# Sample Image Prompts

Adapt these recipes to the user's request without inventing extra story elements or unrequested brands.

## Generate

### `photorealistic-natural`
```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Primary request: candid photo of an elderly sailor on a small fishing boat adjusting a net
Scene/backdrop: coastal water with soft morning haze
Subject: weathered skin with wrinkles and sun texture
Style/medium: photorealistic candid documentary photo
Composition/framing: medium close-up, eye-level
Lighting/mood: soft coastal daylight, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain
Materials/textures: real skin texture, worn wool fabric, salt-worn wood
Constraints: natural color balance; no heavy retouching; no watermark
Avoid: studio polish; staged look
```

### `product-mockup`
```text
Use case: product-mockup
Primary request: studio product photo of a matte black ceramic pour-over kettle
Scene/backdrop: clean studio gradient from light warm gray to white
Subject: single kettle centered with subtle surface reflection
Style/medium: commercial product photography
Composition/framing: slight three-quarter angle, generous negative space for page copy
Lighting/mood: softbox lighting, crisp highlights, controlled soft shadow
Materials/textures: powder-coated matte metal, natural walnut handle
Constraints: no logos or trademarks; no text; no watermark
```

### `ui-mockup`
```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Primary request: mobile app home screen for a local farmers market showing seasonal vendors and daily specials
Style/medium: realistic modern product UI mockup, not abstract concept art
Composition/framing: clean vertical mobile layout with clear card hierarchy and generous padding
Lighting/mood: bright neutral light mode with fresh botanical accents
Constraints: practical typography, legible labels, no placeholder gibberish, no watermark
```

### `infographic-diagram`
```text
Use case: infographic-diagram
Primary request: clean architecture diagram of an API gateway routing client requests to providers
Scene/backdrop: clean off-white canvas
Subject: Clients -> Auth & Policy -> Router -> Providers (Copilot, Codex, xAI)
Style/medium: crisp flat technical infographic with clean rounded boxes and directional arrows
Composition/framing: horizontal slide layout, left-to-right flow
Text (verbatim): "Clients", "Auth & Policy", "Router", "Copilot", "Codex", "xAI"
Constraints: strong contrast, clean alignment, no tiny text, no watermark
```

### `logo-brand`
```text
Use case: logo-brand
Primary request: original minimalist mark for "Field & Flour", a neighborhood bakery
Style/medium: flat vector-style logo mark, geometric simplicity
Composition/framing: single centered emblem on a plain cream background with generous padding
Color palette: warm terracotta and deep charcoal
Constraints: strong silhouette, balanced negative space, original design, no gradients, no watermark
```

### `stylized-concept`
```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Primary request: cavernous orbital hangar interior with a compact shuttle parked near the center
Scene/backdrop: vast industrial bay with tall support ribs and drifting atmospheric mist
Style/medium: cinematic concept art, grounded industrial realism
Composition/framing: wide-angle, low-angle establishing shot

```

## Edit

### `precise-object-edit`
```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Primary request: replace only the background behind the ceramic mug with a warm terracotta sunset gradient
Constraints: change only the background; keep the mug, glaze highlights, handle geometry, and edges completely unchanged; no text; no watermark
```

### `lighting-weather`
```text
Use case: lighting-weather
Primary request: shift the daytime street photo to blue hour after light rain with subtle wet-pavement reflections
Constraints: change only lighting, sky atmosphere, and wet surface reflections; preserve all buildings, vehicles, signage, and framing identically
```

### `compositing`
```text
Use case: compositing
Primary request: Image 1 is the empty sunlit living room base photo; Image 2 is the olive linen armchair. Place the armchair from Image 2 near the left window of Image 1 with matched warm afternoon shadows and perspective.
Constraints: preserve room architecture, floor texture, window light, and camera angle from Image 1; preserve the armchair shape and fabric texture from Image 2
```

### `text-localization`
```text
Use case: text-localization
Primary request: replace the English headline "OPEN DAILY" on the storefront sign with "每日营业"
Text (verbatim): "每日营业"
Constraints: preserve the sign material, perspective, lighting, weathering, and every surrounding detail unchanged
```
